# Font licensing

- Inter is packaged from Debian's `fonts-inter-variable` and licensed under SIL Open Font License 1.1.
- JetBrains Mono is packaged from Debian's `fonts-jetbrains-mono` and licensed under SIL Open Font License 1.1.
- Newsreader is licensed under SIL Open Font License 1.1. Its pinned upstream artifact and checksum are recorded in `config/dependencies.json` before inclusion in a release image.
- DejaVu, Liberation, and WenQuanYi Zen Hei remain installed as Debian-packaged fallback fonts with their corresponding package licenses.

Roobert is not included in the redesigned distributable image because no redistribution evidence is present in this repository.
